Sample Article: How Modern Web Standards Protect Users

Accessibility and privacy are two foundational pillars of the open web. Under modern regulations such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), users must be given clear and informed consent controls before tracking identifiers like cookies are stored.

Simultaneously, the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.2 AA) ensure that people with low vision, cognitive differences, ADHD, or motor challenges can effortlessly consume and navigate digital information. Try clicking on our floating widget to activate the Reading Guide or Dyslexia Font.
